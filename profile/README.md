# Welcome to AIdentifyAGE -- Artificial Intelligence and its Application by Forensic Science Service Providers: Migrant Unidentified Age Estimation :wave:

## What is Project AIdentifyAGE?

The knowledge and the proof of age are indispensable and invaluable for all
human beings. The forensic age estimation (FAE) has played an increasingly
important judicial role, not only in the forensic identification of cadavers,
but also in living individuals, minors and undocumented. FAE may have to be
performed in cases of human trafficking, asylum seekers, child labour, sexual
abuse, conscription of children in armed conflicts, juvenile delinquency, and
crime.

The main goal of this first project is to use the range of age until 23 years
old with dental growth staging techniques.  the development of a new
methodology to fill this gap is of great scientific importance and practical
application. This study is to show emerging applications of deep learning
technology in a new field: DAA imaging on 2D data from panoramic radiographs.

AIdentifyAGE project received funding from Fundação para a Ciência e a Tecnologia (FCT),
under grant [2024.07444.IACDC](https://doi.org/10.54499/2024.07444.IACDC).
